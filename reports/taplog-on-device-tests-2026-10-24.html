The brand, defined

This page is drawn from the app's own tokens — Sources/TapLog/Theme/Theme.swift — so what you see here is what ships on the phone. The system is a warm field journal: paper grounds, one sage accent, botanical linework, and a single serif voice reserved for focal numbers.

Palette

Every colour is adaptive: two hex values per token, one per appearance, chosen pair by pair so each keeps its contrast in both modes. Over-budget uses clay, never red — the budget system encourages rather than judges.
