# Clear TapLog's legal path before 25 September

Apple's own account machinery, not national law, decides whether TapLog ships on 25 September 2026. Before the $4.99 "TapLog Pro" purchase can even load for a reviewer, you need four things in sequence: an approved Apple Developer Program enrollment (in India, only through the Apple Developer app, with no published approval time), a signed Paid Apps Agreement, a W-8BEN tax form that cannot be edited after you submit it, and a bank account that App Store Connect shows as Active. Forum reports of enrollment alone range from 7 to 40+ days. For an offline app that collects no data, the legal exposure is small and fixable. No Indian, US or EU financial licence applies to a manual expense log. India's DPDP duties do not start until about 13 May 2027. CCPA does not apply. GDPR attaches mainly to support emails. The fixes that do matter are concrete. Move the listing name away from the bare "TapLog", which another developer already holds. Take "siri" and "action button" out of the keywords. Add an in-app privacy-policy link, and add your name, address and a support-email section to the policy. Replace "One payment, yours for good". Check that the widget and share extension each carry a privacy manifest. Make a deliberate EU trader decision, because a trader's address, phone and email appear on EU product pages. A Chartered Accountant is needed now for one locked choice: the W-8BEN treaty line. The CA is needed later for GST and the presumptive-income scheme. A lawyer is optional for launch, but you should have one for the name and passing-off risk, for any "not a trader" declaration, and to confirm the privacy analysis before May 2027.

So host the privacy and support pages on a domain you already control, for example under `amteshwar.dev`.

## Apple's account chain puts 25 September at risk

The critical path runs in a fixed order, and Apple publishes a turnaround time for almost none of it. **In India, enrollment is available only through the Apple Developer app.** You must use the same device from start to finish. Your Apple Account must have two-factor authentication and your exact legal name, you need a photo of a government ID, and the fee is 99 USD a year in local currency ([Apple: Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/); [Apple: Enrolling with the app](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/enrolling-in-the-app/)). India is an exception on payment: developers based in India can pay with Apple Account balance from gift cards, which helps if a card is declined ([Apple: Enrolling with the app](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/enrolling-in-the-app/)). The only delay cause that Apple documents is a name that does not match your legal name, such as an alias or nickname ([Apple: Enroll](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)). Forum reports are anecdotal and mostly do not state a region. They show approvals pending for **7, 10, 12 and 40+ days** ([Forum 820213](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/820213); [Forum 813941](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/813941); [Forum 813862](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/813862)). In November 2025, Indian developers reported app errors that blocked enrollment for 4+ days ([Forum 807524](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/807524)). If your enrollment is not yet approved, it is the single biggest threat to the date.

After approval, the Account Holder signs the Paid Apps Agreement. This signature is irreversible, and you need it both to submit an in-app purchase and to apply to the Small Business Program. Next comes the tax questionnaire, which steers a non-US individual to a W-8BEN. Then comes the bank account, which Apple processes only after the tax forms are submitted ([Apple: Sign agreements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/sign-and-update-agreements/); [Apple: Provide tax information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/); [Apple: Enter banking information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-banking-information/enter-banking-information)). Apple's official best case for banking is **"within 24 hours"**. Developer reports describe anywhere from a few days to several weeks in "Processing" ([Forum 810848](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810848)). The bank account holder name must match the bank record exactly, in English letters and punctuation included ([Apple: Banking reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/banking-information/)). Only after all three show Active will TapLog Pro load in sandbox. The most common first-submission failure is products that do not load on the reviewer's device, which leads to a Guideline 2.1 rejection ([Apple TN3186](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3186-troubleshooting-in-app-purchases-availability-in-the-sandbox); [RevenueCat Community](https://community.revenuecat.com/sdks-51/ios-rejection-as-iap-are-not-loading-7719)).

This chain creates an awkward fact: **the W-8BEN sits on the critical path to review, and App Store Connect locks it once you submit it.** Corrections go through Apple support ([Apple: Provide tax information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/)). Book a short CA consultation now, not after launch (see the adviser table below).

The EU trader decision is also on the path to submission. Apple prompts for a status the next time you submit a new app ([Apple: DSA trader requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/)). With a paid unlock, declaring "not a trader" is hard to defend. It would also put a notice on the EU listing that consumer-protection rights do not apply. Declaring "trader" means you verify an email and a phone by two-factor code and upload a current document that shows your name and address. Apple then publishes those details once the app is live in any EU storefront. A P.O. box is acceptable for this display address, with proof such as a receipt or bill. A P.O. box is not accepted for the enrollment address ([Apple: DSA trader requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/); [Apple: Enroll](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)). No source confirms whether a trader who excludes the EU must finish verification before submission, and Apple does not publish how long verification takes. **The pragmatic launch choice is to declare trader status and deselect the 27 EU storefronts in Pricing and Availability for 25 September.** Start the verification now, and switch the EU on once it clears. This keeps the declaration truthful, keeps your home address off public pages at launch, and costs only the EU storefronts for a few weeks ([Apple: Manage availability](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-apps-availability/manage-availability-for-your-app-on-the-app-store)). The address you choose for the DSA display should also be the one in the privacy policy, so the two public identities match.

**Go/no-go checkpoint (my inference, not an Apple rule).** If the Paid Apps Agreement, the tax forms and the bank account are not all Active by about 23 September, you have two options. You can move the date. Or you can submit version 1.0 without TapLog Pro: remove every paywall entry point, so that no hidden or disabled purchase flow remains for a reviewer to find under Guidelines 2.1(b) and 2.3.1. Then add TapLog Pro in 1.1. Apple requires the first non-consumable to be submitted together with a new app version in any case ([Apple: Submit an In-App Purchase](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-in-app-purchase)). The option you must not take is to submit with an IAP whose agreement chain is still "Processing" and hope that review goes well.

## Conclusion

The legal risk profile of TapLog follows from its architecture. Because nothing leaves the device, almost every privacy regime shrinks to one data flow: support email. That section of the policy carries the legal load, and it is the part the draft left out. Because TapLog moves no money and gives no advice, financial regulation does not apply. The real exposure moves elsewhere: to Apple's gatekeeping (enrollment, the agreement chain, metadata rules) and to the brand, which is crowded in the stores, has no domain, and has no trademark yet. The single most leveraged action today is to start every Apple account step in parallel and to book the CA call for the W-8BEN, because those are the only steps whose timing you do not control.

The 25 September date is achievable only if enrollment is already approved or approves within a day or two. Even then, it depends on Apple's banking review. Set the go/no-go at about 23 September, and choose deliberately between moving the date and shipping 1.0 without the purchase. A slip of one or two weeks costs little. A rejected first submission, a locked tax form with the wrong treaty line, or a public home address on EU storefronts are harder to undo than a late launch.
