TapLog Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

TapLog does not collect your data. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. It makes no network requests of its own.

What TapLog stores, and where

The expenses you log (amount, category, note, date), your categories and budget targets, and your settings. All of it stays on your iPhone, in storage that only TapLog, its widget and its share extension can read.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Purchases. Apple handles the TapLog Pro purchase. TapLog learns only whether Pro is unlocked. It never sees your payment details.
  • Siri. When you log with Siri, Apple processes your speech under Apple's own privacy policy. TapLog receives only the amount, category and note that result.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, your TapLog data is part of that backup. Apple or your computer holds it, not TapLog.
  • Exports. A CSV export goes only where you choose to send it.

Notifications

The weekly recap reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service is involved.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all entries removes every expense. Deleting the app removes everything TapLog stored.

Children

TapLog is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
