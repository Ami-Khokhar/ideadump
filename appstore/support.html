TapLog Support

Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Log from the Action Button

Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → choose Open Expense Capture from TapLog.

Log with Siri

Say "Log chai in TapLog" (use any of your category names), or "Log an expense in TapLog". Siri asks how much.

I bought TapLog Pro on another iPhone

Settings → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Move my data to a new iPhone

Your data moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer. You can also export a CSV from Recap → Export CSV at any time.

Privacy
