TapLog — App Store pre-launch checklist

Everything between "the code works" and "it's on the App Store." Researched against current (2026) Apple requirements and cross-checked against the repo's own deep legal report (reports/TapLog App Store legal checklist.md) and appstore/metadata.md. Repo status flags reflect what is actually in the tree today.

The single biggest risk is not code review — it's Apple's account chain. In-app purchases do not load in sandbox or in review until three things are all Active: Paid Apps Agreement → tax forms (W-8BEN) → bank account. Each has no published SLA; reports range from days to weeks. If enrollment isn't already approved, start every account step today and set the go/no-go checkpoint per the timeline at the bottom.

ActionWhy / rule
Complete/confirm enrollment in the Apple Developer app (India: app-only). Two-factor ON, name matches ID exactly.Enrollment is the longest uncontrolled delay. A name/alias mismatch is the one documented delay cause.
Create the App Store Connect record today: "TapLog: Expense Tracker" (23/30 chars). Have a fallback name ready.The bare name "TapLog" is already taken on the US store by another developer (Peter Underwood, "One-Tap Activity Tracking"). Record creation both tests and reserves the string.
Sign the Paid Apps Agreement the day approval lands.Gates IAP + Small Business Program.
CA call → then submit W-8BEN (PAN as foreign TIN).Without a valid form Apple withholds 30% in the US. The form locks; the treaty line (Art 12 royalties / Art 7 business profits / none) is a decision the repo's legal report explicitly flags as CA-territory.
Add the bank account — INR, exact holder name, bank's address.Processed only after tax forms. India/INR payout threshold is effectively nil ($0.02).
Apply to the Small Business Program the same day.15% instead of 30% commission. Takes effect 15 days after the fiscal month of approval — every day of delay can leave September sales at 30%. New developers qualify (≤$1M/yr).

2The EU trader decision (do not skip)

Every developer must declare a DSA trader status — with a paid IAP, "trader" is effectively mandatory (selling counts as Apple's first trader factor). Trader verification publishes your address, phone, and email on EU product pages.
ActionNotes
Declare trader status and start verification now (email code, phone code, name-and-address document; P.O. box OK for display with proof, not for enrollment).Recommended pragmatic path per the legal report: declare trader, then deselect the 27 EU storefronts in Pricing & Availability for launch. Switch the EU on once verification clears. Keeps the declaration truthful and your home address off public pages at launch.
Use the same address for the DSA display and the privacy policy.The two public identities must match.

3Legal fixes to the listing and the app

3.1 · In the app (one string change + one row)

ActionWhy
Change "One payment, yours for good" in PaywallView.included to something like "One-time purchase. No subscription. Restore anytime."Apple's terms grant a licence, not ownership; California AB 2426 restricts ownership wording for digital goods unless the licence is disclosed. Keep "ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION" in the description (factual), never "lifetime"/"own" in UI or product name. The IAP display name in metadata.md ("TapLog Pro") is already clean.
Add a "Privacy Policy" row in Settings linking the hosted policy.Guideline 5.1.1(i) requires the in-app link, not just the ASC field — even for an app that collects nothing.
Add a Terms row in Settings linking Apple's Standard EULA (no custom EULA needed).Legal report item 1.17; costs one row and closes a 2.1(a) surface.
Add a one-line "not financial advice" disclaimer in Settings → About (and on the support page).SEBI hedging per legal report item 1.18. Recaps must describe spending, never recommend.

4Privacy manifests & the privacy label (binary-level, since May 2024)

No PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy exists in this repo. App Store Connect rejects uploads with undeclared required-reason API usage (ITMS-91053 family). TapLog's three targets all use UserDefaults; CSV export touches file timestamps.
ActionNotes
Create PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy for the app target: declare NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults with reason CA92.1 (app's own defaults) and 1C8F.1 (App Group shared defaults); declare FileTimestamp with C617.1 if CSV/file APIs qualify; NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = empty; NSPrivacyTracking = false.Third-party SDKs: none (zero dependencies) — so only the first-party bundles need manifests.
Create manifests for TapLogWidget.appex and TapLogShare.appex — each executable bundle that touches UserDefaults (App Group / @AppStorage) needs its own.Extensions are separate bundles; the app's manifest does not cover them. Wire into the three targets via project.yml resources, then xcodegen generate.
Archive → run Xcode's Generate Privacy Report and check all three bundles appear.Catches a bundle silently missing its manifest before upload.
App Privacy label in ASC: "Data Not Collected" — answer No to every collection question.On-device data, Apple-handled payments, and Apple's Siri processing are not developer-collected. Matches the app's real behavior (zero network). The privacy-info section of metadata.md already says this.
Age rating questionnaire: answer "None" everywhere → expect 4+.No web access, no UGC, no gambling, no medical. New 13+/16+/18+ tiers apply to new apps; TapLog's content is 4+ under them.

9Explicitly out of scope (verified non-issues)

  • Financial licences (RBI/SEBI/PSD2/CFPB): no bank connections, no money movement, no advice — non-issues. Hold the design line: recaps describe, never recommend.
  • Demo account for review: the app has no accounts. Review notes explain this explicitly.
  • GDPR Art 27 representative: not required at this scale (occasional, low-risk; controller only for support email) — revisit with a lawyer if EU revenue grows.
  • DPDP (India): main duties start ≈13 May 2027; the policy fixes above keep you ahead of it.
  • Third-party SDK privacy manifests: zero dependencies — first-party bundles only.
