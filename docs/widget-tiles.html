How it learns

The widget queries your last 90 days of entries and groups them by (category, amount) pairs. ₹10 and ₹10.50 are bucketed together. The top 3 most frequent pairs become one-tap tiles. The ₹? button deep-links into the app with the amount field focused.

What changed

Before: Two hardcoded buttons (☕️ $4.50, 🍽️ $12.00).
After: Three learned tiles + custom amount button.

Tiles update every 15 minutes (widget refresh cycle). New patterns emerge as you log. The ₹? button opens the app via taplog://log deep link.

How to add the widget

  1. 1 Go to the Simulator home screen (Hardware → Home)
  2. 2 Long-press on an empty area (or right-click)
  3. 3 Tap the + button in the top-left
  4. 4 Search for "TapLog"
  5. 5 Select the medium widget (shows tiles)
  6. 6 Tap "Add Widget"

Amount rounding

Groups similar purchases:
• Under ₹10 → exact match
• ₹10–50 → round to nearest ₹5
• ₹50–200 → round to nearest ₹10
• Over ₹200 → round to nearest ₹50

So ₹10.00 and ₹10.50 both map to ₹10 — same habit, same tile.
