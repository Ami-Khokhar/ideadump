Mascot Research

What types of mascots work for iOS apps — and what fits TapLog

1. Mascot Archetypes in Popular Apps

Duolingo / Headspace

The Guide / Companion

A character that walks alongside you. Duolingo's owl coaches you through lessons. Headspace's orange blob guides meditation. The mascot IS the experience — it has personality, emotions, and a voice.

Reddit / Discord

The Mascot Logo

The mascot IS the brand mark. Reddit's Snoo, Discord's Clyde. Simple, scalable, works at 16px and 1024px. The mascot doesn't "do" anything — it just IS the identity.

Fintech (Mint, YNAB)

The No-Mascot Approach

Most finance apps avoid mascots entirely. They use abstract marks, gradients, or typography. The reasoning: money is serious, mascots feel childish.

Fitbit / Apple Watch

The Abstract Companion

Not a character — a shape. Apple's Activity Rings, Fitbit's leaf. The "mascot" is a geometric element that responds to your behavior. It's alive without being a creature.

Headspace / Calm

The Animated Element

A simple shape that moves, breathes, and reacts. Headspace's dot morphs and bounces. It's not a character with a face — it's a living shape that creates emotional connection.

Todoist / Things

The Minimal Mark

A clean icon that becomes synonymous with the app. Todoist's checkmark, Things' circle. No character, just a symbol that users learn to associate with the experience.

3. What Fits TapLog's Zen Philosophy

TapLog is NOT a game. The mascot should NOT be:
  • A character with eyes/mouth (feels like a game, not a tool)
  • A "pet" that needs feeding (violates the 5% cognitive load cap)
  • Something that talks or has opinions (too much personality for a finance tool)

What FITS TapLog:

Best Fit

🌊 The Ripple

TapLog already HAS a mascot — the concentric ripple rings from the logo. They breathe, expand, and respond to your logs. The ripple IS the mascot: alive, responsive, zen, zero cognitive load.

Runner Up

🪨 The Zen Stone

The circular amount field is already a "stone" — a zen garden rock. A mascot could be a small animated stone that sits on the home screen, breathing with the ripples.

Alternative

💧 The Droplet

A single drop that creates the ripple when you log. It falls into the stone, creates concentric rings, then dissolves. Simple, physical, satisfying.

4. Recommendation

Don't add a separate mascot. Instead, enhance what already exists:
  1. The ripple rings are already your mascot — they breathe, expand, and respond
  2. The zen stone is already your character — it has physical presence, shadow, depth
  3. On-log, add a droplet animation — a small drop falls into the stone, creating the ripple burst

This is the "Abstract Companion" archetype (like Apple Watch rings) — alive, responsive, zero cognitive load. It fits TapLog's zen philosophy perfectly.
