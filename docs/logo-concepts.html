Why A (The Tap) is my pick: it's the only concept that names the product's *behavior*, not just its category — a tap is the action, the ripple is the log hitting the ledger. It's the most zen (three shapes, no text), the most distinctive against the sea of green-circle finance icons, and it degrades beautifully to monochrome for the widget and share-sheet glyphs. The coin is the safe choice if we'd rather scream "money tracker" than "5-second capture".
