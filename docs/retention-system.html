I Winnable Arenas

Global leaderboards don't work for expense tracking — nobody cares about being #482,912. The winning arena is you vs. your own average.

Weekly Personal Challenge

  • Mechanic: "Stay under $X this week" — auto-calculated from your 4-week rolling average + 10% buffer
  • Why it's winnable: It's your own data, your own baseline. You're not competing with strangers — you're competing with last-you.
  • Transient: Resets every Monday. No permanent rankings, no shame in losing.
  • Visual: A subtle bar on the home screen: "This week: $187 / $220 target" with a sage fill that stops at the target line.
Design rule: The target is always achievable — calculated from YOUR history, not an arbitrary number. If you never spend more than $200/week, your target is ~$180, not $50.

No Game Currency

🛡️ Protection rule

5% Budget

Any new feature must take ≤5% of interaction time. If it requires a tutorial, it's too complex.

Never add: Virtual pets, inventory management, daily quests, energy systems, or "check in to earn coins." These are the S-Curve traps that kill productivity tools.

III Flexible Consistency

Rigid daily streaks are anxiety engines. Expense tracking doesn't happen every day — you don't buy coffee on Sundays. A weekly rhythm with flexibility is the right model.

Weekly Log Streak

  • Target: User-customizable (default: log at least 5 days/week). Options: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
  • Streak: Consecutive weeks where you hit your target. Displayed as "🔥 12-week streak"
  • Streak Freeze: Earned every 10 logs (not purchased). Stored as a consumable — use one to skip a week without breaking the streak. Max stored: 3.
  • Grace Period: If you miss a week, the next week starts at "1" (not "0") with a "Welcome back — log one expense to pick up momentum" nudge.
  • No shame: Missed streaks are never displayed. The counter only shows the current positive streak.
Why 5/week (not 7): People don't spend money every day. A 7-day target guarantees failure for most users. 5/week means weekends off, travel days off, "nothing to log" days off — and the streak still feels earned.

Streak UI

The streak lives on the home screen, below the status line, as a subtle single line:

🔥 12-week streak · 4/5 days this week

Small, always visible, never demanding. The "4/5" creates the Zeigarnik itch — one more log to hit the target.

VII Micro Social Validation

No friend lists, no feeds, no competitive rankings. Just one-tap sharing of genuine financial insight.

Monthly Summary Card

  • Trigger: At month end, a beautiful card appears (or from the Recap tab).
  • Content: Total spent, top category, streak count, comparison to last month.
  • Design: Clean, zen-styled card — looks like a financial report, not a game achievement.
  • Share: 1-tap export to iMessage, Instagram Stories, or WhatsApp. No "Post to Facebook" dialogs.
  • Privacy: The card shows your data only. No friend comparisons unless both opt in.
The social signal: "I track my expenses" is a status signal in itself. The card validates financial discipline, not gaming achievement. That's the kind of thing people actually share.

Implementation Phases

Phase 1

Foundation — Weeks 1-2

The minimum viable retention layer. Zero new UI — just data collection and a single home screen element.

  • Weekly log count tracking (days with ≥1 log per week)
  • Weekly spending ring on home screen (60pt, below status line)
  • Customizable weekly target (Settings → 3/4/5/6/7 days)
  • Basic streak counter (consecutive weeks hitting target)
  • Streak freeze: earned every 10 logs, max stored 3
Phase 2

Rewards — Weeks 3-4

The weekly insight reveal. Variable rewards that make ring completion feel like an event.

  • Weekly Insight card (appears when ring completes)
  • 4 insight types: Category Spotlight, Week Comparison, Pattern Discovery, Milestone
  • Variable weighting: 60% common, 30% uncommon, 10% rare
  • Haptic + ripple burst on ring completion
  • "Almost there" glow at 80% ring fill
Phase 3

Competence — Weeks 5-6

Real financial awareness metrics. The "you became better at this" layer.

  • Monthly spending trend (vs. previous month)
  • Category breakdown with trend arrows (↑↓)
  • "Recurring expense" detection (same category + similar amount, 3+ weeks)
  • Milestone notifications: 50th log, 100th log, first $1,000 tracked
  • Competence score (internal, drives insight quality — not displayed as points)
Phase 4

Social — Weeks 7-8

One-tap sharing of genuine financial insight. The kudos engine.

  • Monthly summary card (zen-styled, data-rich)
  • 1-tap share to iMessage / Instagram Stories / WhatsApp
  • "Beat your average" personal challenge (weekly bar on home screen)
  • Optional: share your streak count (social proof of discipline)
  • No friend lists, no feeds, no competitive rankings — ever
