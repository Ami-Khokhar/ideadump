What we heard

The brief

A local-first iPhone expense logger. Capture a purchase in seconds from the Action Button, a widget, or Siri, and watch weekly budgets grow into a grove of trees. It should feel calm, private and botanical — the opposite of blue fintech dashboards that demand a bank login.

calmprivatebotanicalfastwarm

Success: distinct from blue finance marks, legible at 16 px, works in one colour, and visibly connected to the grove metaphor the app already uses.

Concept A

Leaf

A leaf rooted inside the ring.

  • Keeps the ring equity of the current app icon, so this is an evolution and not a replacement
  • At 16 px it degrades to a ring and a solid centre — exactly the legibility shipped today
  • The ring is a tap ripple and a tree growth ring at the same time
  • Two colours, one-colour safe, 99/100 construction audit

Sprout

  • Weakest at 16 px: the sprout fills in and reads as a keyhole

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

Leaf. It is the only one of the three that keeps its meaning at every size: the ring already exists in the shipped icon, and the leaf adds the grove story without costing legibility. Sprout tells the best story but cannot survive the 16 px app icon; Notch is the safest but loses its idea when small. Recommended next step: take Leaf to a full kit, with a simplified drawing reserved for 16 px if needed.

  • Choose a direction (or name what you like in each).
  • We refine it: geometry, optical corrections, lockups.
  • Colour and typography options for the chosen direction.
  • Final artwork, variants and mini guidelines.
