TapLog: GTM & Lifetime Paywall Strategy

A lean, zero-subscription playbook designed for privacy-conscious iOS power users.

🎯 Executive Summary & Core Wedge

The Thesis: Do not market TapLog as a generic "budgeting app" or "personal finance tracker." Competing on broad feature sets (bank sync, complex graphs, debt tracking) invites support burden and feature bloat. Position TapLog strictly around capture friction and complete privacy.

Core Positioning Statement:

"The private, two-tap iPhone expense log for people who manually track spending—without bank connections, logins, or monthly subscriptions."

  • Target Wedge: iOS Power Users, iPhone 15/16 Pro Action Button adopters, privacy-conscious individuals, and manual loggers escaping heavy SaaS subscriptions.
  • Distribution Edge: Native iOS integration (Prebuilt App Shortcuts, Action Button, Siri, zero configuration).
  • Monetization Model: Generous free utility + Paid Lifetime Unlock (No SaaS/Subscriptions).

Global Market Rationale: Standard micro-utility price point that bypasses software evaluation fatigue and converts instantly via Apple Pay.

Why One-Time Lifetime Beats Subscriptions Here

  • Zero Ongoing Cloud Cost: The app is 100% local-first (CoreData/SQLite). You incur zero server or AI token costs per transaction.
  • Anti-SaaS Resonance: Users are exhausted by ₹299/month subscriptions for simple utilities. A clean "buy once, own forever" model stands out aggressively.
  • High Word-of-Mouth: Indie communities champion apps that respect ownership over recurring rent.

⚖️ The Paywall Architecture: Free vs. Lifetime Pro

Product Principle: The magic (speed, capture, shortcuts) is 100% Free so users build the daily habit. Power-user control, synthesized analysis, and data ownership sit behind the Lifetime Paywall.
Feature Area Free Tier (The Daily Hook) Lifetime Pro (₹299 / ₹499)
Speed & Capture Unlimited manual logs, Action Button, Siri Included + Haptic personalization
Categories 6 Default core categories Unlimited custom categories & icons

Data Ownership In-app transaction history Full CSV / Spreadsheet export
Customization Default system theme Custom OLED dark themes & App Icons

Why Recaps Belong Behind the Paywall

  • Data Value Compounding: On Day 1, a recap has zero value. By Day 14, after logging 40+ expenses, synthesized breakdown is high-value intelligence.
  • The "Awareness" Upgrade Trigger: Free logging establishes the muscle memory. When the user asks *"Where did my money actually go this month?"*, the paywall appears at the exact moment of peak intent.
  • No Black-Hole Effect: Free users still see basic daily/weekly totals so the app never feels broken or crippled.
